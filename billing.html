How these three documents differ

Tax Invoice

A legal document for a supply of goods or services. It carries your GSTIN, a unique invoice number, HSN or SAC codes, place of supply and the GST charged, and is used by the buyer to claim input tax credit.

Estimate / Quotation

A price proposal sent before the work or sale. It is not a tax invoice, carries no tax liability, has a validity date and terms, and the customer may accept it.

Payment Receipt

Acknowledges money actually received. It records the amount, mode, reference and what the payment is for, and is not a tax invoice.
