GST slabs in India (GST 2.0)

RateApplies to
0%Exempt and essential items
5%Merit goods and services
18%Standard rate for most goods and services
40%Luxury and demerit goods
3% / 0.25%Special rates (gold and silver / rough diamonds)

Rates changed with GST 2.0, so the old 12% and 28% slabs no longer apply. Always confirm the rate for your product on the official GST portal.

Types of GST

CGST

Central GST, collected by the Centre on sales within a state.

SGST

State GST, collected by the state on sales within the state.

IGST

Integrated GST, charged on sales between states and on imports.

UTGST

Union Territory GST, charged in union territories along with CGST.

Frequently asked questions

What are the GST rates now?

After GST 2.0 the main slabs are 0%, 5%, 18% and 40%, with special rates of 3% and 0.25% on a few items.

How is GST calculated?

GST amount = amount × rate ÷ 100. To remove GST from an inclusive price, divide by (1 + rate ÷ 100).

How is EMI calculated?

EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ ÷ ((1+r)ⁿ − 1), where r is the monthly rate and n is the number of months.

Is income up to ₹12 lakh tax-free?

Yes, for resident individuals under the new regime. Taxable income up to ₹12 lakh gets a full rebate under Section 87A (Section 156 of the Income-tax Act, 2025). With the ₹75,000 standard deduction, salaried people pay zero tax up to about ₹12.75 lakh. Special-rate income such as capital gains is not covered.

How is SIP return calculated?

Future value = P × [((1+i)ⁿ − 1) ÷ i] × (1+i), where i is the monthly rate and n the number of monthly instalments.
