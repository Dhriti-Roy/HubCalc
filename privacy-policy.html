Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CalcHub (the “Platform”), owned and operated by Dhriti Roy (“we”, “us”, “our”), treats information in connection with your use of this website. By using the Platform you acknowledge the practices described below.

1. Information you enter into the calculators and billing tools

All calculations, and all invoices, estimates and receipts, are processed locally in your web browser. The figures, names, addresses, GSTIN and other details you type are not transmitted to, stored on or accessible to our servers. PDF files are generated on your own device. We do not require you to create an account.

2. Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the hosting infrastructure (Vercel Inc.) may automatically record technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested and the date and time of access in server logs. This data is used to deliver the website, maintain security and diagnose faults. We do not use it to identify you personally.

3. Local storage

The Platform stores your light or dark theme preference in your browser’s local storage. This stays on your device and may be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

4. Cookies and advertising

The Platform may display advertisements served by Google AdSense and other third-party advertising partners. These partners may use cookies, web beacons and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Platform and other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com) or at www.aboutads.info. Where the law requires, we will seek your consent before non-essential cookies are used.

5. Third-party services

To operate, the Platform loads resources from third parties: web fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com), the jsPDF library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com for creating PDF files, and hosting from Vercel. When your browser requests these resources, those providers receive technical information such as your IP address and are governed by their own privacy policies.

6. Purpose and sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. Information is shared only with the service providers named above to the extent necessary to operate the Platform, or where disclosure is required by applicable law or a lawful order of a competent authority.

7. Retention and security

We do not retain personal data that you enter into the tools. Server logs are retained by the hosting provider in accordance with its own retention practices. We use reasonable technical measures, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections, to protect the Platform, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

8. Your rights and grievance redressal

To the extent applicable under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may request access to, correction of or erasure of any personal data we hold about you, and may raise a grievance. Please write to hubcalc@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy Request”. We aim to respond within a reasonable period.

9. Children

The Platform is intended for users aged 18 years and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. External links

The Platform may link to external websites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those websites.

11. Changes to this policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date, and continued use of the Platform constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
